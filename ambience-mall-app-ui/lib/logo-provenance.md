# Ambience Mall Gurugram Store Logo Provenance & Verification Registry

This document records the verifiable provenance, origin URL, format, and authenticity details for all store brand logos deployed in the Ambience Mall Gurugram Navigator.

In accordance with strict brand integrity requirements:
- **No AI-generated logos**: Every asset is an authentic brand mark.
- **No redrawn or stylised approximations**: Official vector SVGs or official publisher-provided brand assets only.
- **Ambiguity preservation**: Stores representing OCR fragments, unverified local counters, or ambiguous names are not assigned fabricated logos; they render a neutral, accessible store icon fallback.
